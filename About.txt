================================================================================
DayFlow - Enterprise Human Resource Management System (HRMS)
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
DayFlow is a modern, full-stack Human Resource Management System built to streamline
workforce governance, shift attendance tracking, leave/permission approvals,
payroll processing, smart workforce analytics, and enterprise compliance.

Designed with a high-contrast, professional light-mode SaaS aesthetic, DayFlow 
provides seamless self-service experiences for employees alongside powerful administrative 
and HR command controls.

KEY FEATURES & MODULES
----------------------
1. Authentication & Security (Phase 2):
   - Secure JWT-based session management with role-based routing (Admin, HR, Employee).
   - Password reset and email verification workflows.

2. Employee Dashboard (Phase 3):
   - Real-time shift clock-in / clock-out widget.
   - Leave balance breakdown cards (Paid, Sick, Unpaid).
   - Recent request status tracking data table.

3. Profile Management (Phase 4):
   - Employee profiles with personal details, work title, department, contact info, and emergency contacts.

4. Attendance Portal (Phase 5):
   - Daily shift recording with working hours calculation.
   - Shift status badges (Present, Half-day, Absent, Leave).
   - Monthly performance metrics and filterable history logs.

5. Leave & Time-Off Workspace (Phase 6):
   - Leave request submission with Start/End Dates and mandatory From Time / To Time fields.
   - Multi-tab queue pipeline for Admin/HR (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
   - Detailed modal reviews with administrator feedback logging.

6. Admin Dashboard & HR Command Center (Phase 7):
   - Live workforce snapshot (Total headcount, active participation rates, leave counts).
   - Quick command shortcuts for adding employees, reviewing leaves, and managing payroll.

7. Employee Directory & Governance (Phase 8):
   - Gated CRUD operations restricted exclusively to Admin roles.
   - Dual layout rendering: Desktop structured table & mobile-responsive card view.

8. Payroll & Salary Governance (Phase 9):
   - Gross salary budgeting, statutory deductions (PF & Income Tax TDS).
   - Net disbursement analytics and interactive payslip modal generation.

9. Smart Insights & Workforce Alerts (Phases 10 & 11):
   - Automated organizational health observations and department availability tracking.
   - Real-time severity risk alerts (Critical, Warning, Info) with action triggers.

10. HR Help Center & Knowledge Base (Phase 12):
    - Policy documentation shortcuts, searchable FAQs, support ticket submission.

11. Notifications & Reports (Phases 13 & 14):
    - Category-filtered real-time notification feeds.
    - Exportable attendance, leave utilization, and payroll analytics reports.

TECHNOLOGY STACK
----------------
- Frontend: React.js, React Router, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Google Fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans)
- Backend: Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB / Mongoose, JWT (JSON Web Tokens), BcryptJS
- Deployment: RESTful API architecture with CORS & environment security.
